## What is the shortest reliable route to Endu?

1. Save at the first Crystal Monument and collect the Dagger Belt along the coast.
2. Use a throwing dagger on the burial-area door switch.
3. Descend to collect the [Endu Shrine Gate Key](/guides/endu-shrine-gate-key/).
4. Return to the coastal shrine, unlock its gate, and save before the fight.
5. Defeat the [Shrine Guardian](/bosses/shrine-guardian/), then claim Endu from the shrine.

This is an early progression route, not a reason to clear every neighboring dungeon first. The official game description identifies Endu as the sword used to fly between platforms. The Steam achievement **Flying Sword** marks collecting it as a separate milestone.

## What should you try after getting it?

Practice Wind Dash where a missed jump is harmless. The sword changes traversal as well as combat, so a gap that looked like a dead end may now be reachable. If a crossing still seems too wide, do not assume you are using the button incorrectly: additional Wind Gems are part of the exploration loop. The [Endu item reference](/items/holy-sword-endu/) explains that distinction.

Return to the island's earlier junctions and inspect routes that ended at open air. For a broader sense of progression, use the [where-to-go guide](/guides/where-to-go-next/) as a milestone list rather than treating it as the only possible route.
