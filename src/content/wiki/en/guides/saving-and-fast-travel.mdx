## Does reaching a Crystal Monument save automatically?

No. Use the monument's **Save** action deliberately. If you die before saving again, you return to the last saved state. After a key item, a difficult route, or a boss victory, saving is usually the safest next action.

Resting is separate: it restores health and clears negative effects, but it also respawns enemies. If you only need to preserve progress, decide whether you want that respawn before selecting Rest. This distinction matters during the [first hour](/guides/first-hour/) and before the [Shrine Guardian](/bosses/shrine-guardian/).

## Which travel option takes you where?

| Option | Destination | What to check first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wing Altar | Another discovered Wing Altar | You must have found a usable altar. |
| Wing Stone Fragment | A Wing Altar from your current location | This is a consumable. |
| Travel Crystal Fragment | Your last saved Crystal Monument | Check where you last saved. |

Do not use a fragment merely because you are lost; confirm its destination and whether you still need to save. If you are stuck in a hard area, the [Verdant Hollow escape guide](/guides/escape-verdant-hollow/) applies these options to a common early mistake.

## What is the safest routine?

Save after collecting a progression item, then explore. When you encounter a new monument, save again before trying a dangerous route. If you rest for healing, expect the path back to contain enemies. The [route guide](/guides/where-to-go-next/) helps choose an objective after you return.
