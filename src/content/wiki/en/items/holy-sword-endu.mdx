## Where do you get Endu?

Follow the [opening Endu walkthrough](/guides/get-holy-sword-endu/): collect the shrine key, open the coastal gate, defeat the [Shrine Guardian](/bosses/shrine-guardian/), and take the sword. Steam's **Flying Sword** achievement explicitly tracks collecting Holy Sword Endu.

## What does the sword change?

The official Steam description says Endu lets you fly from platform to platform. In practice, that makes it a route-opening tool. Revisit visible gaps around the first area after obtaining it, and practice the dash over safe ground before making a long jump.

The game also mentions upgrades that let the sword fly farther. A failed crossing may indicate a later upgrade or a different launch point. Use the [where-to-go guide](/guides/where-to-go-next/) to pick another path instead of retrying the same gap indefinitely.

## Is Endu only useful as a combat weapon?

No. Its movement role is the reason to keep track of it even if you prefer another weapon for a fight. Player discussions also describe evolution paths for Endu, but specific late-game forms should be verified in your current game version before following a long upgrade route. See the [weapon-evolution guide](/guides/weapon-evolution/) for the bounded community evidence.
