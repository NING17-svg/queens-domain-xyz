## Where is the key?

The key is not at the shrine gate. It lies in the burial area off the opening route. Start with the Dagger Belt near the coastal wreckage, then follow the path back toward the underground stone rooms. At the blocked entrance, aim a dagger at the diamond-shaped target beside the door.

Inside, descend toward the lower chamber and take the pickup labeled **Endu Shrine Gate Key**. The side rooms are optional for this immediate objective, although a Wooden Club is available on this route if you want another damage type for the upcoming fight.

## What should you do before leaving the burial area?

Open any return shortcut you encounter and make another manual save when you reach a Crystal Monument. That shortens the retry after a failed shrine attempt. If the entrance does not respond, check that the Dagger Belt is equipped and that you are aiming at the switch rather than striking the stone door.

Take the key back along the coast to the gated shrine. The [Endu walkthrough](/guides/get-holy-sword-endu/) continues from that point, including the guardian and the first movement upgrade.

## Is the key the same thing as Endu?

No. The key opens access to the shrine. Endu is the sword obtained after the [Shrine Guardian](/bosses/shrine-guardian/) encounter. The distinction matters when deciding which obstacle you are trying to solve: a locked gate needs the key; a distant gap needs the sword's Wind Dash. The [first-hour checklist](/guides/first-hour/) covers the preparation before either.
