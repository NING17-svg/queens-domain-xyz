## What is your next useful milestone?

| If you have reached... | Try next |
| --- | --- |
| The opening coast, without the shrine key | Find the burial route and [collect the key](/guides/endu-shrine-gate-key/). |
| The locked Endu Shrine | Open it with the key, then prepare for the [Shrine Guardian](/bosses/shrine-guardian/). |
| Endu, but no clear route | Revisit gaps around the opening area and learn the sword's Wind Dash. |
| A region where enemies are far stronger | Save, look for a return route or travel point, and choose another branch. |

Steam's achievement list separately records collecting Endu, reaching Obmuer Temple, reaching Verdant Hollow, reaching the Tower of Knowledge, and reaching Deimpo Mausoleum. These are useful landmarks. Their unlock percentages do **not** establish a required order: Queen's Domain has connected routes and players can explore them differently.

## What if a gap still looks impossible?

First make sure you have [Holy Sword Endu](/items/holy-sword-endu/) and understand the aerial dash. Then look for another approach or later movement upgrade. Spending a long time retrying an obviously distant gap is rarely as useful as marking it mentally and exploring another branch.

## What if you are lost in a dangerous branch?

Use a discovered Wing Altar or a suitable travel fragment where available. The [saving and fast-travel guide](/guides/saving-and-fast-travel/) explains destinations and costs. Players who dropped into Verdant Hollow too early can use the [escape guide](/guides/escape-verdant-hollow/).
