## Where does the club fit in the opening?

Look for it while descending through the burial rooms on the way to the [Endu Shrine Gate Key](/guides/endu-shrine-gate-key/). You do not need to abandon your current weapon; the club simply gives you another damage type and a different attack rhythm to test before the [Shrine Guardian](/bosses/shrine-guardian/).

## Why do players keep it?

Multiple player discussions report that the Wooden Club has an evolved form with a healing ability. That makes it attractive for long exploration stretches where recovery is more valuable than a small damage gain. This is a community-observed benefit; the exact level and effect should be checked against your own item description and current game version.

If you are deciding where to spend weapon time, compare it with the [weapon-evolution overview](/guides/weapon-evolution/). For traversal progression, do not substitute it for [Holy Sword Endu](/items/holy-sword-endu/): the sword opens gaps, while the club is a combat and survival choice.
