## Does every weapon evolve?

No. Community reports describe a difference between leveling a weapon and changing it into a named form. Players repeatedly identify the Wooden Club, Holy Sword Endu, and Rusted starter weapons as evolution candidates. That is a useful shortlist, not a verified complete catalogue of every item in the game.

| Weapon family | What players report | Practical decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Wooden Club](/items/wooden-club/) | An evolved form with a healing ability | A useful early investment if exploration survival is your problem. |
| [Holy Sword Endu](/items/holy-sword-endu/) | Further forms as the sword levels and its quest advances | Keep it in use for movement even while testing another main weapon. |
| Rusted weapons | Several evolve into new forms | Keep the one whose reach and damage type suit your fights. |
| Other weapons | Many simply gain level-based damage | Read the item description before assuming an evolution exists. |

## Which one should you work on first?

If you are still learning combat, the Wooden Club is a practical candidate because players report its later healing effect. Endu is a separate progression item because its Wind Dash opens routes regardless of your preferred combat weapon. The [first-hour guide](/guides/first-hour/) explains why combat rhythm and manual saves matter before farming weapon levels.

Avoid treating any community ranking as universal. A weapon's reach, damage type, and your ability to land a safe hit matter more than a single reported damage number. The [Shrine Guardian guide](/bosses/shrine-guardian/) is an early example of choosing a comfortable attack window.
