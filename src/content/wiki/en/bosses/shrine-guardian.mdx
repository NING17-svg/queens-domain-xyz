## How should you prepare?

Bring the [Endu Shrine Gate Key](/guides/endu-shrine-gate-key/) from the burial area and save at a Crystal Monument before entering. A firsthand opening guide identifies the Wooden Club's impact damage as a useful option for this fight. It is an option, not a requirement; use the weapon whose reach and timing you understand.

Make sure the Dagger Belt has charges if you plan to attack from range. The [first-hour guide](/guides/first-hour/) explains the Power Meter: repeated rushed swings leave you with less effective attacks and less room to respond.

## What is the safest attack pattern?

Keep space behind you. When the guardian lifts its sword overhead, retreat farther than you would for a normal swing. After it finishes an attack, step in for one deliberate hit and move out again while the Power Meter recovers. You can add a throwing dagger when there is room to aim safely.

Do not commit to a charged attack if you need to move immediately. A slow, repeatable opening is better than trading damage for a faster fight. If you lose, your last manual save determines how much of the approach must be repeated.

## What happens after the fight?

Claim [Holy Sword Endu](/items/holy-sword-endu/) inside the shrine. The sword's Wind Dash is the important progression reward: it changes which gaps you can cross around the island. The [Endu route](/guides/get-holy-sword-endu/) shows where this fight fits in the opening sequence.
