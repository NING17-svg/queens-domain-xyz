## What should you do first?

Treat the opening as preparation for one objective: reaching the Holy Sword Endu. Save at the Crystal Monument near the starting route, collect a reusable ranged option, and take the key from the burial site before approaching the shrine. The [Endu route](/guides/get-holy-sword-endu/) gives the route in order if you want directions.

The island has no conventional map. Notice the coast, the first monument, and the shrine gate as landmarks. If a path leads to enemies that overwhelm you, leaving it for later is reasonable; the world has more than one connection.

## How do you avoid losing progress?

Saving is manual. Interact with a Crystal Monument and choose **Save** after a useful pickup or before a difficult fight. **Rest** heals and removes negative effects, but it also brings enemies back. Those are different decisions, so do not assume a rest has protected the last stretch of exploration. See the [saving and travel guide](/guides/saving-and-fast-travel/) before using a travel item.

## What changes early combat?

Wait for the yellow Power Meter to recover between deliberate melee strikes. Holding an attack charges it, but charging limits what else you can do at that moment. Against a new enemy, learn its reach, make one safe hit, and step back rather than emptying the meter with repeated swings.

The Dagger Belt gives you a ranged attack and also interacts with switches on the opening route. Its charges replenish, so it is useful for testing suspicious targets without spending a finite consumable. The [shrine-key guide](/guides/endu-shrine-gate-key/) explains the first such gate.

## When are you ready to leave the opening area?

You are ready to challenge the shrine once you have saved, found the key, and have a melee weapon you can handle confidently. The [Shrine Guardian guide](/bosses/shrine-guardian/) covers the fight without assuming a particular level. After collecting Endu, return to crossings you could not reach before.
