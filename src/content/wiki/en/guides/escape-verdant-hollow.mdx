## How do you get out without clearing the enemies?

The safest reported option is to find Verdant Hollow's Wing Altar, a large circular travel structure, and use it to return to a previously discovered altar. If you have a Wing Stone Fragment, it can provide an altar route from your current location. These options matter when the enemies on foot are too strong to fight reliably.

Another item, the Travel Crystal Fragment, returns to the **last saved Crystal Monument**, which may or may not be where you intended to go. Check your last save before consuming it. The [travel guide](/guides/saving-and-fast-travel/) compares all three methods.

## Why does this happen so early?

An interconnected world lets you enter branches before you are comfortable fighting there. A player discussion describes reaching a Verdant Hollow monument soon after finding the Endu shrine key and discovering that the local enemies were far stronger than expected. That is a route problem, not a sign that you must defeat every enemy before leaving.

Once back, continue the [opening Endu route](/guides/get-holy-sword-endu/) if you have not claimed the sword yet. If you already have it, the [milestone guide](/guides/where-to-go-next/) helps choose a more manageable branch.
